# Image transitions using the GPU

Go ahead — play with it. Swap the effects.  Every variation runs live at 60fps, no pre-rendered video, no GIF, no smoke and mirrors.

::demos-image-transitions-tweakpane
::

What you're seeing isn't a CSS animation or a simple crossfade — it's a **GLSL shader running directly on your GPU**, manipulating every single pixel on screen in real time.

CSS can fade, slide, or scale. It cannot bend light, distort space, or ripple pixels based on noise functions. The effects here are computed thousands of times per frame at the hardware level — something no browser stylesheet will ever be capable of.
